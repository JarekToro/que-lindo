The signature interaction

Switching modes is one animated reflow, not a screen change. The frame never moves. Cards keep their identity and their order and only change what they encode — equal width becomes true length, seams appear, the audio lane rises from zero height and the music starts. That single transition teaches the idea of duration without a word of copy, which is why it is the one motion this surface owns.

Arrange — equal widths, no lane
Mid-reflow — cards stretching, lane rising
Time — true lengths, lane playing

Capability ledger — hidden, never removed

Two permanent panes become zero. Every control reachable in the app today is accounted for below; an unlisted control would be a deletion, and deletion is an anti-goal.

TodayWhere it livesWhy
Media bin (250px, permanent) Gone. Import lands on the collapsible Not used shelf; photos enter the film by drag or by Build slideshow, and dragged-out cards drop back onto it. You cannot reorder what is not in the film.
Inspector (300px, permanent) Gone. Selection summons a contextual panel of 3–5 outcome-level controls; raw values behind a disclosure. The user owns order and grouping. Everything else is chosen for them and stays adjustable.
Filmstrip (fixed strip, always proportional-ish) Becomes the timeline, which carries the mode. The mode is a property of the timeline, not of the window.
Layout picker · margin · gutter Contextual panel, Arrange outcome-level Named results (“Featured”, “Even grid”) with numbers behind Values ▸.
Cells per slide (add / remove a photo from a collage) The band, Arrange — opening a group card expands it across the grid. ×, drag, or ⌫ lifts a photo out as its own slide directly after the group; drop a card onto a group to add one. Down to one member, the group dissolves. Grouping is one of the user's two decisions, so its inverse must be as reachable as the bind.
Per-cell fit · motion · corner · border Contextual panel, Arrange outcome-level “Zoom in”, “Pan →” rather than from/to crop windows.
Transition type · duration The seams, Time Placed where the transition physically occurs.
Slide duration Card width, Time — draggable edge The quantity is the control.
Text overlays: content, timing, fades Contextual panel, Time Wording and timing are temporal decisions.
Text overlays: anchor, offset, wrap Direct manipulation on the frame, Arrange Placement is spatial. Drag, don't type coordinates.
Audio tracks: gain, fades, seek, loop Audio lane, Time Only meaningful once the music is audible.
New · Open · Save · Save As · resolution · Presets · Export Top bar — unchanged Outside the scope of the compositional change.
Space / ← / → transport · ⌘Z undo Unchanged, plus keyboard reorder and grouping 300 items must be operable without a mouse.
Not decided by this schematic: the exact card size and column count at 1100px minimum width, the disclosure pattern behind “Values ▸”, the keyboard grammar for reorder, how the band lays out 7–8 members, and how the grid keeps the opened group's row in view while the cards below it reflow.
Sequencing note: audio playback in preview is not built and gates Time mode's honesty; it is the largest single engineering item here and its responsiveness constrains the mode's design, so it precedes the UI work. Proportional scrubbing across hundreds of slides needs frame caching or throttling on top of render_preview, and progressive import needs probe_media to stop awaiting one file at a time.
